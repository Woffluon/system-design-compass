## What changed, and why

Observed omissions drove each revision. Guidance makes retry identity and authorization concrete, resolves in-flight transactions before ownership transfer, distinguishes durable application from capture, and requires a repair path for migration discrepancies. Queue estimates separate unfinished business operations from retry-amplified attempts. Recommendations include a concrete failure injection, observable business outcome, and pass/fail criterion.

The entrypoint routes narrow decisions directly and broader work to its relevant mode. It instructs the agent to search headings and read only the section needed for the current decision, avoiding a full workflow or reference bundle for a narrow ADR. Existing interview guidance, 28 workload topics, and 14 references remain available. Evaluation tools live outside the distributable skill.

Across revisions, the entrypoint moved from **`594` words (`original`)** to **`450` words (`rc.9`, `-24.2%`)**, **`469` words (`rc.10`, `-21.0%`)**, and finally **`397` words (`1.1.0`, `-33.2%`)**, achieving the approximate `400`-word target while preserving required correctness rules.

## Comparison boundaries

The Antigravity provider comparison uses the user-selected `gemini-3.6-flash-medium` and `gemini-3.8-flash-medium`, both at `medium` effort through Antigravity CLI `1.2.14`. Because both are Flash variants, they do not establish a distinct strong/smaller model-tier comparison. Codex results remain separate and are never paired with Gemini baselines.

Each subject starts in a fresh temporary project with identical raw fixtures inline. Expected solutions and scoring checks are withheld. A restricted workspace agent allows evidence reads, forbids delegation and other installed skill bodies, and retains native and normalized traces. Global rules and the skill catalog remain cumulative: there is no OS-level containment or claim that Antigravity removed its common prefix.

Grades use anonymous answer IDs, immutable completed answers, and two-case batches. The grader has no tools and receives no condition or model names or earlier scores. Answer prose can still reveal skill use; label withholding is not perfect blinding. Every critical check must pass, at least `80%` of all checks must pass, and unsafe behavior fails the case. Automated grading is not independent human validation.

## Measurement discipline

Skill-read words and characters measure returned tool text, including heading searches and partial reads. They are approximate context proxies, not token counts. Reference counts measure distinct references. Repeated reads can include a heading index followed by a decisive section; inspect traces before calling them waste.

Native input, output, cached-input, and thinking fields are reported separately with their available-record counts. Fields have different provider semantics; adding cached or thinking counts to input/output does not establish a billing total. The common Antigravity prefix can dominate total input even when skill reads shrink.
