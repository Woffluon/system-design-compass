## What changed, and why

Observed omissions drove the revision. Guidance now makes retry identity and authorization concrete, resolves in-flight transactions before ownership transfer, distinguishes durable application from capture, and requires a repair path for migration discrepancies. Queue estimates separate unfinished business operations from retry-amplified attempts. Recommendations include a failure injection, observable business outcome and pass criterion.

The entrypoint routes narrow decisions directly and broader work to its relevant mode. It instructs the agent to search headings and read the section needed for the current decision. This avoids requiring a full workflow or reference bundle for an ADR. Existing interview guidance, 28 topics and 14 references remain available. Evaluation tools live outside the distributable skill.

The current entrypoint is **450 words, down from 594 (24.2%)**. The approximate 400-word target was not achieved; required correctness rules were retained. This count alone does not establish lower loaded context.

## Measurement discipline

Skill-read words and characters measure returned tool text, including heading searches and partial reads. They are approximate context proxies, not token counts. Reference counts measure distinct references. Repeated reads can include a heading index followed by a decisive section; inspect traces before calling them waste.

Native input, output, cached-input and thinking fields are reported separately, with their available-record counts. Missing usage is unavailable. Fields have different provider semantics; adding cached or thinking counts to input/output does not establish a billing total. The common Antigravity prefix can dominate total input even when skill reads shrink.
